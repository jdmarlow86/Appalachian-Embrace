FAQ

Questions are welcome.

Is Appalachian Embrace a dating service?

No. It is a professional, strictly platonic companionship service.

Do I have to cuddle?

No. Cuddling is never required. You decide what is comfortable within the service guidelines.

Who can book a session?

The planned service is for adults 18 and older. Identification and other eligibility requirements can be established in the final policies.

Can I change my mind during a session?

Yes. Consent is ongoing. Either person may pause or end a session at any time.

Where do sessions take place?

The service location and coverage area are still being finalized and will be clearly posted before booking opens.

Is this a medical or mental-health service?

No. Appalachian Embrace is not therapy, medical care, or a substitute for professional health services.
